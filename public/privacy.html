PRIVACY AND CONTROL

Your Mac.
Your control.

ClawdTime works on your Mac only when you ask. This page says how to stop it, what it keeps, and what leaves your Mac.

Updated 28 November 2026

How to stop it

  • Press Stop in the bar at the top of the screen. The bar says “ClawdTime is using this Mac” while it works.
  • Hold the Escape key.
  • Say or type “stop” in the chat.

It answers: “Stopped. An action that was already sent may have finished.” A stopped task does not start again by itself.

What it asks before

It asks for your yes before a payment, a delete that you cannot undo, a change to account security, a grant of access, and terms that bind you. It asks before it sends or posts, unless your request already said so, such as “reply to Sam”. A yes is for that one action. A missed call or no answer is never a yes.

You type every password and code yourself. ClawdTime never types into a password field.

What it keeps on your Mac

Everything is in ~/.local/share/clawdtime, in files for you only:

  • The last 30 tasks: your words, and the kind and target of each step. Never a picture, the text that it typed, or the text of a page.
  • The chat, your routines and your model keys.
  • Your memory, only while memory is on.

Pictures of the screen are never written to disk.

What leaves your Mac

  • To the model provider that you chose, for each action: your request, a picture of the screen, the names of the things on the screen, and the text of a page when it reads one. Close what you do not want in the picture before you start a task.
  • To OpenAI, when you talk: your voice while it turns into text, and what you said.
  • Through Chert, for phone calls (preview): your calls and texts, and the screen when you ask to see it on a call.

Your passwords, codes and card numbers are never sent to a model and never kept. Each key goes only to its own provider.

The demo video

The homepage loads a picture from YouTube. When you choose Play, it loads the YouTube player from youtube-nocookie.com. YouTube then uses its own policies.

What this website counts

When counting is on, the website counts page views and clicks on the Download and Copy buttons, for each day. It keeps the page name, the kind of site that you came from (such as a search engine), the button and its place, and a count. It keeps counts for 90 days.

It uses no cookies and no visitor ID. It does not keep full page addresses, the link that you came from, or what you type. It skips bots, and it follows Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track.
