SCREEN ACCESS / DATA

Your screen.
Your permission.

ClawdTime separates calling, screen viewing and computer input. The pilot shares only after explicit setup and local permission, and provides a local Stop control.

ClawdTime team · Checked November 13, 2026

The demo video

The homepage loads a thumbnail from YouTube. Choosing Play loads YouTube's embedded player from youtube-nocookie.com. YouTube then processes the playback request under its own policies. You can also open the video directly on YouTube.

What can be shared?

You choose the Mac display and authorize the paired session. A selected display may include every visible app on that display; it is not application-level isolation. Close private material before enabling sharing.

Screen viewing does not grant input permission. Computer input needs a separate supported control mode, macOS permission and current session authorization. The connected agent keeps its own native approval policy.

How do I stop it?

Use Stop sharing and control in the Mac Control Panel. Local Stop, lock and sleep end ClawdTime capture/input. Call-bound sharing ends with the call. Cancellation of a native tool already running is cooperative and depends on that tool; a stop request is not proof its action was undone.

Mute and End call are separate controls. Reconnecting does not silently restore a revoked screen selection.

What data does the pilot handle?

Data Handling
Screen frames and audio Transient media, not saved by ClawdTime as recordings. Provider processing is part of the call path.
Pairing and authorization Operational records bind the intended member, device, project and session and support revocation.
Delivery and action outcomes Receipts and uncertain outcomes are retained to avoid duplicate calls, messages or inputs.
Your coding agent and Messages Those applications and their providers have their own handling and retention. ClawdTime’s transient-media behavior does not erase their history.

We do not claim that nothing is stored or that a submitted input proves an action succeeded. Ask your pilot contact about the configured services before sharing sensitive material.

Optional website measurement

When enabled, the public website counts page views and selected CTA clicks in daily aggregates. It stores a fixed public-page name, per-page referrer category, button placement, pilot availability and count. Reports cover the past 90 days. Older counts are removed when a new event or report is processed.

This measurement uses no cookies, persistent visitor IDs, raw referrers or full URLs. It does not collect phone numbers, pairing tokens, message text, screen/audio content or project paths. It excludes the operational setup and pairing pages, ignores recognized bot user agents, and honors Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals.

Counts are not unique visitors or verified human activity. An opened Messages draft is not a sent message or completed setup. There is no session replay.

Questions about your pilot?

Contact the person who issued your invitation for setup, access or data-handling questions. Keep personal links and credentials out of public repository issues.
