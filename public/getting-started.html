GETTING STARTED / PUBLIC GUIDE

A guided start.
Your existing tools.

ClawdTime is an invited Mac pilot. Your pilot contact provides the trusted build and private setup instructions. This page explains prerequisites; it does not activate your Mac, claim an invitation or place a call.

ClawdTime team · Checked November 13, 2026

Before you begin

  • An invitation and the intended iMessage/FaceTime handset.
  • A Mac running macOS 14 or later, with Node 24 or later.
  • The trusted ClawdTime companion and an operator-provisioned gateway.
  • A supported, running coding session and the project you intend to connect.
  • A display you are comfortable sharing, with private material closed or hidden.

Public npm installation, notarized public downloads and automatic enrollment of a new Mac are not available yet. Do not guess an install command or download a similarly named package.

Set up with your pilot contact

  1. Read the instructions supplied with your invitation. Keep the personal link private. Do not paste it into a public issue, screenshot or repository.
  2. Give the setup prompt to the intended coding session. Use the approved local build. Your agent guides configuration while preserving its native permissions.
  3. Select the screen and grant permission locally. Screen Recording is needed for viewing. Accessibility and separate control consent are needed for supported computer input. View-only is a valid choice.
  4. Enable the companion locally. Check the selected display and session in the Mac Control Panel. Being connected is not itself permission to share.
  5. Authorize one supervised test call. Confirm the correct screen, response delivery and continuation with your pilot contact. If the call fails or has an unknown outcome, do not repeatedly redial.

How do I know setup worked?

A successful installation or connected call is only a milestone. In the supervised test, check that the phone shows the intended screen, your feedback reaches the intended agent, and the agent actually continues the agreed work. A computer input being submitted is not proof that the intended UI changed.

Local Stop ends ClawdTime sharing and input. End the call when finished. Stop or contact your pilot operator if the wrong screen, session or device appears.
