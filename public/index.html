WHAT IT DOES

Say what you want.
ClawdTime does it on your Mac.

Ask in plain words.

Click the mascot to open the chat. Type, or press Talk and speak. Your next words change the task.

You say

Find a phone case on shop.example and put it in the cart.

It uses your apps for you.

It opens apps, clicks, types and reads the screen. An orange cursor shows each move before it acts.

You say

Open Stonepad and make a note: call the dentist on Monday.

It calls you when it needs you. Preview

When a task needs your yes or your sign-in, it can call your phone and ask. A missed call is never a yes.

ClawdTime asks

Place the order on shop.example? It costs 48 dollars. Say yes or no.

It also does a task at a set time, such as “every morning at 9, check my inbox and tell me what is new”. It remembers what you tell it when you turn memory on. Phone calls are a preview: today they need a phone number from Chert and the source install.

ONE COMMAND

Paste one command.

For Terminal users. It installs for you only, with no sudo. You do not need Node, Git or Xcode.

  1. Open Terminal.
  2. Paste the command. Press Return.
  3. Answer the setup questions. Then click the mascot and ask.
$ 
curl -fsSL https://www.clawdtime.com/install.sh | sh

Run it again to update. Your settings and keys stay.

You need a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later, and a key from a model provider, such as OpenAI or Anthropic. Read the setup guide

YOU STAY IN CONTROL

It asks first.
You can always stop it.

  • It asks before it pays or deletes. It asks you first before it pays, or deletes something for good. It also asks before it sends or posts, unless you asked for that.
  • Your passwords stay with you. You type them on the Mac. ClawdTime never asks for one.
  • You can stop it at any time. Press Stop in the bar at the top of the screen, hold Escape, or say “stop”.
  • No screen recordings. Pictures of the screen go only to the model provider that you chose. ClawdTime never saves them to disk. What leaves your Mac

QUESTIONS

Before you start.

What do I need? +

A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later. A key from a model provider, such as OpenAI, Anthropic or Google Gemini. Voice uses an OpenAI key.

Does it cost money? +

ClawdTime is free to download and use. You pay your model provider for what you use. We plan to offer paid plans for phone calls. See pricing.

Which models can it use? +

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Mistral, Groq, OpenRouter and more, or your own endpoint. You choose in Settings.

What leaves my Mac? +

For each action, your request and a picture of the screen go to the model provider that you chose. Your passwords stay on the Mac, and each key goes only to its own provider. Close what you do not want in the picture before you start a task. Read the details.

Can it call my phone? +

Yes, as a preview. When a task needs your yes and nobody answers in the chat, it calls you once and asks. If the call does not connect, it sends you one text. Today this needs a phone number from Chert and the source install.

How do I remove it? +

In the app, open Settings > General. Choose Uninstall ClawdTime. In Terminal, run clawdtime uninstall. It moves the files to the Trash and asks first.
